# KAABIL — Intelligent Stream Selection Guide (Docs)

## 1. Background & The NEP 2020 Transition

Historically in India, the Class 10 stream selection decision (Science vs Commerce vs Arts) acted as an irreversible life funnel. Students choosing Commerce were permanently barred from scientific careers; students choosing Humanities were deemed "non-quantitative".

Under the **National Education Policy (NEP 2020)**, rigid boundaries between arts and sciences, curricular and extra-curricular activities, and vocational and academic streams are being removed.

The **KAABIL Stream Selection Assistant** (`/stream-selector`) is built to embody this multi-disciplinary flexibility.

---

## 2. The 6-Step Diagnostic Evaluation

The Assistant evaluates student orientation across 6 foundational vectors:

1. **Natural Curiosities & Fascination**: Identifies spontaneous reading and exploration themes (cellular biology, computing logic, capital markets, constitutional justice, spatial design, or agrarian ecosystems).
2. **Thinking & Problem-Solving Archetype**: Determines how the candidate prefers to resolve ambiguity (scientific deduction, mathematical proofs, balance sheet auditing, oral debate, or hands-on prototyping).
3. **Daily Physical Work Environment**: Aligns preferences with real-world operating conditions (sterile hospital wards, R&D tech parks, financial trading suites, courtrooms, design studios, or open-air fields).
4. **Educational Duration Appetite**: Calibrates academic stamina (1-2 year accelerated vocational diploma vs standard 4-year degree vs 5.5 to 8+ year medical/legal gestation).
5. **Core Life Ambition**: Understands primary life driver (healing human pain, technological invention, capital creation, constitutional justice, or public governance).
6. **Linguistic Preference**: Selects preferred language for AI reasoning (English, Hindi, Hinglish).

---

## 3. Trade-Off Analysis (Benefits vs Realities)

Every stream diagnosis presents an unvarnished, transparent trade-off analysis:

- **What Students Love (Benefits)**: Social respect, earning potential, creative autonomy, or job stability.
- **What Students Must Expect (Academic Rigor & Realities)**: Extreme entrance ratios (NEET/JEE competition), continuous recertification, long residency hours, low initial pass rates (CA Final), or high early-career gestation periods.

---

## 4. Alternative Bridge Streams & Transition Pathways

To support undecided students and career switchers, the Assistant provides 2 alternative bridge streams with concrete actionable advice:
- **PCM $\to$ FinTech / Actuarial**: Applying high school calculus and statistics to quantitative algorithmic trading.
- **Arts $\to$ Corporate Law**: Transitioning political science and history into 5-year integrated BA LLB via CLAT.
- **PCB $\to$ Biotechnology / Bioinformatics**: Combining biology with computing infrastructure.

---

## 5. Advisory Disclaimer

In compliance with Indian educational ethics, every diagnostic output clearly displays the advisory notice:
> *"Stream recommendations are advisory tools designed to guide self-reflection under NEP 2020. Students should consult qualified academic counselors, teachers, and guardians before finalizing subject combinations."*
