# KAABIL (काबिल) — Institution-Employer Recruitment Integration

---

## 1. Ecosystem Synergy

KAABIL bridges the historical divide between educational institutions and corporate employers. Rather than operating as isolated silos, institutions (universities, polytechnics, ITIs, colleges) and employers share a unified placement coordination layer.

---

## 2. Campus Placement Coordination Workflow

```
[ Verified Employer ]
        │
        ▼ (Proposes Campus Drive)
[ Institution Console: /institution/placements ]
        │
        ▼ (Training & Placement Officer Approves Drive)
[ Student Opportunity Feed: /student/opportunities ]
        │
        ▼ (Adult Student Voluntarily Registers with DPDP Consent)
[ Employer ATS & Pipeline: /employer/applications ]
```

### Statutory Consent Guarantees:
- **No Unsolicited Data Dumps:** Institutions cannot bulk-export student personal phone numbers or addresses to employers without student consent.
- **Affirmative Registration:** Eligible students receive drive alerts and voluntarily opt in by submitting their verified skill credentials.
