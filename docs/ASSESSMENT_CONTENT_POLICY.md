# KAABIL Assessment Content & Minor Protection Policy
**Document ID:** DOC-D1-05  
**Product:** KAABIL (काबिल) — AI-Powered Universal Career Ecosystem  
**Milestone:** Part D1 (Prompt 12)  
**Status:** Implemented & Verified  

---

## 1. Compliance with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023

1. **Minor Protection Mandate:**
   - Under Section 9 of the DPDP Act 2023, personal data of individuals under 18 years of age (Class 8–10 students) must not be processed in a manner that harms their well-being.
   - For minors, verifiable parental/guardian consent indicators (`guardianConsent`) are tracked.
   - Minor student profiles and assessment scores are strictly excluded from public directory indexing or employer discovery without express verification.

2. **Age-Appropriate Mentor Interactions:**
   - Mentors speaking with Class 8–10 students focus on broad curiosity, foundational problem solving, and exploratory concepts rather than high-stress placement demands or competitive anxiety.

---

## 2. Ethical Content & Anti-Fraud Guardrails

1. **Zero Fake Degrees or Government Licenses:**
   - KAABIL assessments test aptitude, knowledge, and practical comprehension.
   - Passing an assessment awards a KAABIL skill badge; it NEVER claims to award statutory licenses (e.g. MBBS, LL.B., CA, B.Tech, or B.Ed).
   - Marketing materials, student UI, and reports explicitly state: *"Verified competence in core skills. Does not constitute official government licensure or university affiliation."*

2. **Objective Fairness & Stream Neutrality:**
   - Questions in vocational, sports, hospitality, and arts streams are authored with the same pedagogical rigor and cognitive depth as STEM and medical questions.
   - No question relies on derogatory stereotypes regarding educational choices or socio-economic backgrounds.
