## 1. Overview & Regulatory Disclaimers

The Institutional Outcome Reporting module aggregates cohort diagnostic telemetry, workshop completions, intervention resolutions, and placement metrics into standardized supporting reports inspired by leading accreditation and ranking frameworks in India:
- **NAAC Criterion II & V:** Teaching-Learning Continuous Evaluation & Student Support/Progression.
- **NIRF Metrics:** Graduation Outcomes (GO), Placement & Higher Studies (GPH), and Median Compensation.
- **NBA Outcome-Based Education (OBE):** Program Outcomes (POs) and Course Outcomes (COs) Attainment.

### Mandatory Statutory Disclaimer
Every generated report, view, and exported file displays the following statutory notice:
> *"This document is an internal institutional development and supporting analysis report generated by the KAABIL Career Intelligence Platform. It does not replace statutory accreditation submissions or regulatory filings with NAAC, NIRF, NBA, UGC, or AICTE."*

## 2. Report Templates

### 2.1 NAAC Criterion II & V Supporting Analysis
- **Criterion II (Teaching-Learning):** Total enrolled students, continuous skill evaluations conducted, adaptive learning coverage percentage, average cohort Career Readiness Index.
- **Criterion V (Student Support & Progression):** Identified intervention cases, interventions resolved, skill enhancement workshops conducted, placement drives facilitated, verified skill evidences awarded.

### 2.2 NIRF Graduation Outcomes (GO) Supporting Report
- Eligible graduating cohort (adult learners in UG, PG, Diploma, ITI).
- Count of students securing placements or confirmed offers.
- Estimated placement percentage and median readiness benchmark.
- Higher studies preparation tracking.

### 2.3 NBA OBE Attainment Report
- Program Outcomes attainment rates (PO1 Knowledge, PO2 Problem Analysis, PO4 Complex Problem Investigation, PO9 Teamwork, PO12 Lifelong Learning).
- Continuous improvement tracking via resolved interventions and updated curriculum alignments.

## 3. CSV Formula Injection Sanitization (CWE-1236)

To prevent CSV injection attacks where malicious cell values execute arbitrary commands in spreadsheet applications (Microsoft Excel, LibreOffice Calc, Google Sheets), the export utility (`ReportingService.exportReportToCsv` and `sanitizeCsvCell`) strictly neutralizes formula triggers:
- Any cell string beginning with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, `\t`, or `\r` is automatically prepended with a single quote (`'`).
- Embedded double quotes and delimiter commas are safely escaped with standard RFC 4180 quotation syntax.
- All exported files are delivered as UTF-8 encoded CSV attachments with download headers.
